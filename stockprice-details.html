Stock Price Prediction

Project information

  • Category: Data Visualization / Economic Analytics
  • Project date: 2024
  • Skills: Tableau, Data Visualization, Time-Series Analysis, Economic Data, Dashboard Design
Problem Statement

Understanding the long-term value of the US Dollar and its role in global foreign exchange reserves requires analyzing complex historical and economic data across multiple decades. Raw economic datasets alone do not clearly communicate how major global events—such as recessions, financial crises, and policy shifts—have impacted purchasing power and reserve dominance. There is a need for an intuitive visual narrative that connects historical events with economic trends.

Solution Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard that visually explores the historical value of the US Dollar alongside major economic events and global foreign exchange reserve distribution. Using time-series charts, annotated milestones, and interactive visuals, the dashboard allows users to understand how purchasing power has changed over time and how the US Dollar compares globally in terms of reserve holdings. The solution transforms complex economic data into an engaging, story-driven visualization that supports economic analysis and informed interpretation.
